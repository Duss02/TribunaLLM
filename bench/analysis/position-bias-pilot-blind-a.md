# Position bias in the final verdict (60 blind debates)

The same surviving transcripts, the same question, the options listed PRO-first (as in the tournament) and CON-first.

- Mean p(PRO) with PRO first: 0.376; with CON first: 0.434.
- Mean shift in p(PRO) from listing PRO first: -0.059 (sd 0.033, standard error 0.004).
- Leading side changes with the order in 5 of 60 debates (8.3%).
- Rerun drift (same order as the tournament): mean |Δp(PRO)| 0.018, max 0.080.
