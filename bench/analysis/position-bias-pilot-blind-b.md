# Position bias in the final verdict (120 blind debates)

The same surviving transcripts, the same question, the options listed PRO-first (as in the tournament) and CON-first.

- Mean p(PRO) with PRO first: 0.314; with CON first: 0.370.
- Mean shift in p(PRO) from listing PRO first: -0.056 (sd 0.036, standard error 0.003).
- Leading side changes with the order in 9 of 120 debates (7.5%).
- Rerun drift (same order as the tournament): mean |Δp(PRO)| 0.017, max 0.080.
